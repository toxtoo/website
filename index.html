Discover Field Archery

Welcome to Stonebow Field Archers, a National Field Archery Society (NFAS) affiliated club set in the countryside near Thirsk, in the heart of North Yorkshire.

Whether you are a complete beginner eager to pick up a bow for the first time, an experienced archer looking for a new home club, or a visiting member from another NFAS club, you will find a warm, supportive community waiting for you.

Field archery offers a dynamic, immersive experience away from traditional target archery. Our club ground features a woodland course designed to test your accuracy, distance judgment, and adaptability across varied natural terrain and realistic target layouts.
